# Anzi design direction (Oct 2026)

Status: revision 2, 2026-10-11. The owner approved the visual identity of the mock-ups. The product core changed: **the address people see and say is a street and a house number ("12 Rue Manguier"), not the code.** This document replaces the "Visual direction" paragraph of `docs/refresh-plan.md` section 4 and the screen list of that section.

Related documents:
- **`docs/street-addressing.md`** (being written by another agent) owns the street data model, the automatic numbering algorithm, the name confirmation rules and the API. When it disagrees with a rule here, it wins.

Mock-ups: `docs/mockups/home-a.html`, `home-b.html`, `flow.html`. Open them by double-click; add `#long` to the URL to unroll every screen.

Labels used in this document: *measured* means measured in this repo today; *verified* means read on the vendor's page today. Anything else is a design choice or is flagged as unverified.

## 0. Key decisions

1. **The visual identity stays exactly as approved.** It evolves the original template:
   - dark brand frame (app bar, Home hero, success screen and address card);
   - light working surfaces (forms, lists, map);
   - charcoal + orange, the owner's compass-pin logo in a cream badge, Inter + Architects Daughter;
   - warm shadows, CSS-only motion.
2. **Address = street + house number.**
   - Full form: "12 Rue Manguier, Bastos, Yaoundé". Short spoken form: "Manguier 12".
   - The number is shown on a small orange plate everywhere.
   - The code (YDE-7K4M) becomes a small **"code de secours"**: a permanent backup ID, used in links, never the hero.
3. **Neighbours look like neighbours.** On every map:
   - community streets are orange lines with a name chip;
   - house numbers are tags along the line (odd on one side, even on the other);
   - every place card has an "À côté : n°10 · n°14" strip.
4. **Numbers are automatic** (from the position along the street, rules in `street-addressing.md`). Nobody types a house number. The app shows "Votre numéro : 12" with one plain sentence.
5. **Streets are named by people, never officially.**
   - The first person on an unnamed street taps one of 3 easy local words (tree, fruit, bird; French first, English toggle), writes their own word, or skips.
   - The name reads "nom communautaire, provisoire" until about 3 neighbours confirm.
   - Old names keep working after a rename.
   - Streets are a commune competence (Law 2019/024), so the UI never says "official".
6. **The map is the hero and stays cheap.**
   - Home shows a static map image with the street lines and number tags drawn on top by the server (0 kB of map JS).
   - Mapbox GL (261 kB gzip, *measured*) loads only after "Agrandir".
7. **New "Y aller" sheet hands off to a maps app:** the phone's own maps app (`geo:`), Google Maps, Waze, Apple Plans, or copy the position.
8. **Structure:**
   - Bottom bar: Trouver (Home with the map) · Créer (raised orange button) · Mes adresses.
   - Create flow: 5 steps (position, place, **street**, landmark, consent) + success.
   - Find: **one box** for "Manguier 12", "12 rue manguier bastos" or the code.
9. **One primary button style:** orange `#FF8506` with charcoal text (7.37:1). White on orange is never used (2.44:1).

**Recommendation: Home variant A ("Night hero")**, with B's offline state.

## 2. Design direction (unchanged since the approved mock-ups)

### 2.1 Light or dark
- **Light where people read or type:** forms, lists, map, find result. Outdoors, dark text on a light background survives low brightness and glare better.
- **Battery is not a reason for dark:** dark saves power only on OLED screens, and many low-cost Android phones use LCD (the panel mix among users is *unverified*).
- **Dark brand frame:** Home app bar and hero, success screen, the shareable card, and the bottom bar in variant B.
- No automatic dark theme in Phase 1.

### 2.3 Typography
- **Inter** (variable, self-hosted by `next/font`, 47 kB *measured*). Sizes:
  - display 31/34 800;
  - title 26/30 800; **address titles use the same size, with the number on an orange mini-plate**;
  - section 20/26 800; body **16/24**; labels 16 700;
  - meta 14, only for labels under icons, chips and the map attribution.
  - Numbers use tabular figures.
- **Architects Daughter** (13 kB *measured*): eyebrows and notes on the map only, never for information.
- The **code de secours** is set in Inter 700 at 15 px with 0.08em tracking, muted. It is never larger than the body text.

### 2.4 Logo
- **Mark:** the owner's `anzi_V3prime.png`, trimmed to 256 px high (7.5 kB, `docs/mockups/assets/anzi-logo.png`).
- **On light:** as is, at least 24 px high, with clear space of half its width.
- **On dark:** always inside the cream badge (`sun-50`, radius 28%). Sizes: app bar 40, card 40, credits 36.
- **Never** straight on charcoal, recoloured, or replaced by a generic pin.
- **PWA icon:** the logo at 58% height on `sun`, for 192, 512 and maskable 512 (`assets/icon-orange-512.png`, `icon-orange-192.png`).
- **Manifest:** `theme_color #151719`, `background_color #FBF6EF`.

### 2.5 Imagery
- **Main imagery:** the city map (with streets and numbers) and the street-lines motif (an inline SVG pattern under 1 kB).
- **One illustration:** `delivering.png`, cropped and recoloured to the palette and to local skin tones (19 kB webp, `assets/delivery.webp`). Licence unknown (section 9).
- **Later:** photos of local gates and streets from pilot partners, each 100 kB or less.

### 2.6 Iconography
- Inline SVG, 24 px grid, 2 px stroke, round caps and joins, as in `components/app/icons.tsx`.
- Every icon has a visible label, except back and close, which get an `aria-label`.
- **Categories:** home, shop, scissors (salon), fork-and-knife (restaurant), briefcase (office), dots (other).
- **Actions:** search, plus, pin-plus, locate, move, navigate, share, copy, download, flag (landmark), key, users, lock, shield, wifi-off, expand, volume.
- **New for streets:** `road` (street), `tree`, `fruit`, `bird` (name ideas), `phone` (the phone's maps app).
- A generic WhatsApp-style glyph, never the trademark artwork.

### 2.7 Shape, depth, motion
- **Radii:** fields 14, buttons 16, cards 18-24, sheets 28, chips fully rounded, number tags 8, logo badge 28%.
- **Shadows:** warm, tinted with `earth` (two levels). Buttons and plates have a 3-4 px inset bottom lip.
- **Pins echo the logo:** a teardrop with an `earth` outline and a cream disc that now holds the **house number**, plus the base-ellipse shadow.
- **Address plate** (success card): orange, 4 small "screws", the number then the street, like a gate plate.
- **Motion (CSS only):** pin drop 700 ms, "you are here" pulse 2.4 s, sheet rise 450-500 ms, card rise 600 ms, button press 100 ms. All off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 3. Information architecture

| Route | Screen | Bottom bar |
|---|---|---|

- **Links:**
  - Shared links and deep links use the **code** (`/find?code=YDE-7K4M`), because it is unambiguous and survives street renames. The text people read shows the street address.
  - Links to `/map` use `prefetch={false}`, so Home never downloads the map chunk.
- **Bottom bar:** Trouver (search) · **Créer** (raised 62 px orange disc) · Mes adresses (bookmark). The active tab gets a `sun-200` pill and a `clay-700` label.

### 4.3 Find (mock-ups "Find: one box" and "Find: result")
- **One box**, labelled "Adresse ou code". It accepts:
  - "Manguier 12" or "12 Manguier";
  - "12 rue manguier bastos" (case, accents and the word "rue" optional; a quarter or city word narrows the search);
  - an old street name;
  - the code.

  The parser lives in `street-addressing.md`.
- **One match** → the result screen.
- **Several matches** → "{k} adresses « manguier 12 » · Laquelle ?" with up to 5 rows: the number mini-plate, the street, "ancien nom : …" in `clay-700` when the match came from an old name, then "quarter · city · distance". Rows are sorted by distance when location is allowed, otherwise by quarter. A tip suggests adding the quarter or using the code.
- **No match:** the "Find none" string (4.0) with the same tips.
- **Result screen:**
  - app bar with the query;
  - static map (224 px) with the street line, chip, number tags and the selected pin (drop animation);
  - card: category tag + "Nom communautaire" (or "Nom provisoire") chip; **title = "12" mini-plate + "Rue Manguier"**; "name · Bastos, Yaoundé · à 1,2 km" (the name only for public places); neighbours strip; landmark;
  - primary **"Y aller"** (opens 4.4); [Partager] [Copier] (copies "12 Rue Manguier, Bastos, Yaoundé");
  - a small line "Code de secours YDE-7K4M · Copier";
  - growth block "Vous aussi ? Donnez une adresse à votre maison ou boutique."
- **Privacy:** what a link-only home shows to someone who typed its exact address is open decision 2. The mock-up shows a public salon.
- **States:**
  - loading: skeleton card + map motif;
  - offline: answer from this phone's own addresses if the query matches one, otherwise the network message.

## 5. Map spec

### 5.1 Style "Anzi Day"
One custom Mapbox Studio style (free) for both the static images and GL:
- land `#F3ECE0`, water `#A8D1E7`, parks `#D5E8BF`, buildings `#EAE0D2`;
- white minor roads with a `#DCCDB9` casing, `#FFD9A8` major roads with a `#E8BC86` casing;
- labels `#55595F` with a land-coloured halo;
- Mapbox POI icons off.

### 5.2 Streets and house numbers (new)

| Element | Look | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed community street | `sun` line 5.5 px over a 9 px `earth` casing | Drawn on top of the base road |
| Provisional street | `sun` dashes (10/7) over a 7.5 px `earth` casing | The dashes say "not settled yet" |
| Unnamed road (create step 3 only) | `field` dashes 3.5 px (5/6) | Chip "Sans nom" |
| Street name chip | white pill, 1.5 px `earth` outline, Inter 800 13 `earth`, rotated with the street (horizontal if steeper than 30°) | Provisional: dashed `clay` outline + "provisoire" in `clay-700`. Step 3 adds the address count. One chip per street per view, at the start of the street or in a free gap |
| Number tag, anonymous | 30x24, radius 8, `sun-50` fill, `earth` 1.5 px outline and text (13.19:1) | About 20 px off the line, on the house's side (odd one side, even the other) |
| Number tag, public place | `sun` fill, category glyph + number in `night-900` (7.37:1) | Tap → place sheet |
| Number tag, mine | `earth` fill, `sun` 2 px ring, `sun-200` text (11.61:1) | |
| Selected place | 50x66 teardrop, `earth` outline, cream disc with the **number**, drop animation | It hides the tags it overlaps |
| Neighbours (cards) | "À côté" + n°10 — **n°12** — n°14 on small plates joined by an orange dashed line | Not tappable (privacy) |

- **Zoom rule:** number tags only at street zoom (16 and above). Clusters (dark discs with a count) only below 16. **Never both in one view:** both are numbers and would be confused.
- **Placement priority:** selected pin > street chips > public tags > anonymous tags. Lower-priority items that collide are hidden.
- **Static preview (Home, find result, step 3):**
  - the server requests the static image (one stable URL per area, no overlays in the URL);
  - it draws the street lines as an inline SVG overlay and the tags and chips as absolutely-positioned HTML;
  - positions come from Web-Mercator maths (about 15 lines, no library);
  - data comes from the streets API (`street-addressing.md`).
- **Interactive map (`/map`):** GeoJSON line and point sources, styled with the same values. A tag tap opens the React place sheet (street + number first), never `mapboxgl.Popup`.

### 5.3 Budgets and costs
- **mapbox-gl 2.15** (*measured*): 261 kB gzip JS + 5 kB CSS, already over the 250 kB map-route budget. So GL is never in first-load JS. `/map` paints the static preview first and fetches GL after. When `saveData` or 2G is reported, show "La carte utilise environ 300 Ko de données." The Phase 0 spike measures MapLibre (*not measured here*).
- **Free tiers** (*verified* on mapbox.com/pricing today): GL 50,000 map loads/month; Static Images 50,000 requests/month. GL loads only on a tap. The static URL is stable per area, so it caches.
- **Unverified:** the weight of a 360x210@2x static image in this style. If it is over 100 kB, request 1x.
- **The street overlay adds no image weight.** Its payload is a short list of lines and points per view; cap it in the API.

### 5.4 Offline
- No tile or static-image prefetch: OSM policy forbids offline prefetching; check Mapbox terms before caching static images in the service worker.
- The motif placeholder replaces the map.
- "Y aller" via `geo:` still opens an offline-capable maps app.

## 8. Open decisions for the owner

1. **Home variant: A "Night hero" (recommended) or B "Day map".** Both use the same flows.
2. **What a link-only home reveals to someone who types its exact address.** Recommended: position, street, number, quarter, landmark and category only; never the place name, photo or phone. Rate-limit address lookups to stop people walking a street number by number. Say this in the consent text. Align with `street-addressing.md`.
3. **Show the numbers of non-public addresses on the map as anonymous tags?** Recommended: yes, number only, at street zoom, not tappable. Without them a street shows only public places and your own, and adjacency is hard to see.
4. **Who curates the street-name word list?** Recommended: you and the pilot partner, about 60 words (trees, fruits, birds; FR with EN equivalents) such as manguier, safoutier, goyavier, fromager, tisserin, calao. No people, brands, politics or religion. Review it before launch.
5. **Map look: a custom "Anzi Day" style (recommended, free, about an hour in Mapbox Studio) or stock `streets-v12`.** Only the custom style gives the warm look of the mock-ups.
